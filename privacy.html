Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Ideary is a free web app to capture creative ideas, turn them into step-by-step plans with AI, and track progress. This page explains what happens with your information. Short version: we don't collect, store, sell or see your data. Ideary has no servers or databases of its own.

Where your ideas are stored

Without an account: your ideas, notes, sketches and settings are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. They never leave it unless you export them.

With Google Drive connected: your ideas and settings are saved as files ("ideary-data.json" and "ideary-settings.json") in your own Google Drive. Ideary reads and writes them directly from your browser. Nobody else, including the creator of Ideary, can access them.

Google account permissions

When you connect Google Drive, Ideary asks for:

  • Access only to files Ideary creates (Google's drive.file permission). Ideary cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, only to show which account is connected.

Ideary's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This data is used only to provide the app's features to you, is never transferred or sold to anyone, and is never used for advertising or to train AI models.

You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI features (Gemini)

AI features are optional. If you add your own Gemini API key, it's stored in your browser and, if connected, in your own Drive settings file. When you use an AI feature, the text of the idea you're working on is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API using your key, under Google's Gemini API terms. Ideary doesn't receive or keep those conversations.

If you use the "copy prompt" option instead, you choose which AI you paste it into, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Other services

Ideary is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Google Sign-In library from Google. These providers may log basic technical information (like your IP address) as part of serving those files, under their own privacy policies. Ideary doesn't use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

  • In Ideary, open Settings → Your ideas → Delete data on this device to remove everything stored in your browser.
  • To delete your Drive data, delete "ideary-data.json" and "ideary-settings.json" from your Google Drive.
  • To disconnect Ideary from your Google account, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Ideary is not directed to children under 13.
